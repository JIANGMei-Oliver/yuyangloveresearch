Yuyang Jiang

PhD Student in Cognitive Science, HKUST

I am a researcher working at the intersection of affective computing, human-centered AI, and creative technologies. My research explores how computational systems can understand human emotion and support wellbeing, accessibility, and creative expression.

I received an MPhil in Computational Media and Arts from HKUST(GZ) and a Bachelor of Engineering from the China Academy of Art.
